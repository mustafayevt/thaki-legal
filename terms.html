What Thaki is

Thaki helps you turn a goal into milestones, and each milestone into tiny steps you can take today. You can build a plan yourself, or ask Thaki to plan your goal for you.

Free: 3 goals in total, with up to 4 milestones each, whether or not you sign in; deleting or finishing a goal doesn't give one back. A free account also backs up and syncs them. Free has no plans from Thaki. Thaki Pro: unlimited goals, up to 20 milestones each, plans from Thaki (up to 10 a day under fair use, with an account), and three more coats, four more outfits and six more home-screen tiles.

Plans from Thaki are a Thaki Pro feature and need an account. A plan counts once Thaki delivers it, and the Pro allowance is counted over the last 24 hours. Building a plan yourself is free and has no limit beyond the goal and milestone numbers above.
