Common questions

Do I need an account?

No. You can build a goal and walk it on your iPhone without signing in. Signing in with Apple keeps your goals synced, and planning with Thaki needs an account and Thaki Pro.

I got a new phone.

Sign in with Apple to keep your goals on a new phone. Goals on a phone that never signed in stay on that phone; an iPhone backup restores them.

How do I cancel Thaki Pro?

Thaki Pro is billed by Apple, so you cancel it in the Settings app: tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Thaki. Cancel at least 24 hours before it renews. Deleting the app or your account does not cancel it.

I paid and Thaki Pro didn't unlock.

Tap Restore purchases on the Thaki Pro screen (or You → the profile card → Account → Restore purchases). If it still doesn't unlock, email us. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I turn off the reminder?

Switch it off in You → Reminders. Thaki sends one nudge a day at most, and only if you turned it on.

How do I delete my account?

You → the profile card → Account → Delete account. The goals on your phone stay.
