Contact us

Email badambura.corp@gmail.com. We're a very small team, so please allow a few business days for a reply. It helps enormously if you include:

  • your device and iOS version,
  • what you expected to happen and what happened instead,
  • roughly when it happened (so we can line it up with our error reports).

Please don't send passwords or sign-in codes — we will never ask for them.

Common questions

Do I need an account? No. Thaki works fully offline and signed out — your trails, camps, and daily steps live on your device. Signing in (an emailed code, no password) only adds sync so your trails follow you to another device.

I lost my trails / I got a new phone. If you were signed in, sign in again with the same email and your trails sync back down. If you were never signed in, your trails only ever existed on the old device — restore it from an iCloud/iTunes device backup, or use Settings → Export a backup before switching next time.

How do I export or import my data? Settings → "Export a backup" writes a JSON file you can save or send anywhere. "Import a backup" restores one. This works signed out.

How do I manage or cancel Trail Pro? Subscriptions are billed by Apple, not by us, so they're managed in your Apple ID: iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Thaki. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid the next charge. Already paid for a subscription on another device? Trail Pro screen → "Restore purchases".

I was charged and Trail Pro didn't unlock. Tap "Restore purchases" on the Trail Pro screen first — that re-checks your Apple ID. If it still doesn't unlock, email us. Refunds are handled by Apple (<https://reportaproblem.apple.com>); we can't issue them ourselves.

How do I turn off the nudges? Settings → "Gentle nudges". They're reminders only — Thaki never shames a missed day, and a lapsed streak just quietly starts counting again.

How do I delete my account and my data? Settings → "Delete account". You'll be asked whether to keep the trails on this device or wipe them too. Server-side data is deleted by our backend before the account itself, and the app won't claim a deletion the server didn't confirm. See the Privacy Policy for the full picture of what we store.

Accessibility

Thaki respects the system Reduce Motion setting throughout, and every control carries a VoiceOver label. If something is unusable with an accessibility setting turned on, please tell us — we treat that as a bug, not a request.
