Thaki — Support

Last updated: 2026-09-19

Contact us

Email badambura.corp@gmail.com. We're a very small team, so please allow a few business days for a reply. It helps enormously if you include:

  • your device and iOS version,
  • what you expected to happen and what happened instead,
  • roughly when it happened (so we can line it up with our error reports).

Please don't send passwords or sign-in codes — we will never ask for them.

Common questions

Do I need an account? No. Thaki works fully offline and signed out — your paths, camps, and tiny steps live on your device. Signing in (Sign in with Apple, or an emailed code, no password) only adds sync so your paths follow you to another device.

I lost my paths / I got a new phone. If you were signed in, sign in again the same way (Sign in with Apple, or the same email) and your paths sync back down. If you were never signed in, your paths only ever existed on the old device — restore it from an iCloud/iTunes device backup.

How do I manage or cancel Thaki Pro? Subscriptions are billed by Apple, not by us, so they're managed in your Apple ID: iPhone Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions → Thaki. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid the next charge. Already paid for a subscription on another device? Thaki Pro screen → "Restore purchases".

I was charged and Thaki Pro didn't unlock. Tap "Restore purchases" on the Thaki Pro screen first — that re-checks your Apple ID. If it still doesn't unlock, email us. Refunds are handled by Apple (https://reportaproblem.apple.com); we can't issue them ourselves.

How do I turn off the daily reminder? Settings → "Daily reminder". It's a morning nudge to take one tiny step, nothing more — Thaki never shames a missed day, and a lapsed streak just quietly starts counting again.

How do I delete my account and my data? Settings → "Delete account". This removes your account and its server-side rows. If you just want to wipe the copy on this device without deleting your account, use Settings → "Start fresh" instead. See the Privacy Policy for the full picture of what we store.

Accessibility

Thaki respects the system Reduce Motion setting throughout, and every control carries a VoiceOver label. If something is unusable with an accessibility setting turned on, please tell us — we treat that as a bug, not a request.
