Thaki: Goal Trails — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-07-19

The short version

Thaki is local-first: your trails, camps, and daily steps live on your device whether or not you ever create an account. We don't run ads, and we don't sell your data — to anyone, ever. If you sign in, a minimal set of data syncs to our backend (Supabase) so your trails follow you across devices; AI trail-building sends your goal's title (not your journal entries or personal notes) to a third-party AI provider through our own server. The details are below.

1. What Thaki stores locally, on your device

Everything you do in Thaki — every goal, milestone ("camp"), daily todo, check-in, streak, and preference — is saved to a single local data file on your device. This works fully offline, before you ever create an account, and continues to work if you never do. Deleting the app deletes this data; we don't hold a hidden copy of it unless you've turned on cloud sync (§2).

2. What syncs to our servers, if you sign in

Thaki uses Supabase as its backend. Signing in is always optional — the app is fully usable without it.

  • Account & authentication. Signing in currently uses an email address

and a one-time code we send to it (no password to remember). We store your email address and Supabase's authentication records for your account.

  • Synced goal data. If you're signed in, your goals, milestones, todos,

check-ins, and streak sync to our Supabase database so they're available if you reinstall the app or use it on another device. This is the same data described in §1 — sync doesn't add new categories of data, it just gives your existing data a second home.

  • Usage events. We record a small set of product-usage events (things

like "app opened," "goal created," "camp conquered") to understand how people actually use Thaki and to fix what's confusing. These events are keyed to your account if you're signed in, or to a random, anonymous per-install identifier if you're not — an identifier that isn't tied to your name, email, or device in any way we can reverse. If you later sign in, your anonymous history and your account become linked going forward; we don't retroactively deanonymize anything beyond that. Event data is retained for a limited window (on the order of months) and then deleted.

3. AI trail-building — what leaves your device and why

When you ask Thaki to chart a trail for you (instead of building it yourself), we send your goal's title — for example, "Run a 10K" or "Learn to bake sourdough" — to an AI model through OpenRouter, a service that routes requests to various AI providers. We don't send your check-ins, journal-style notes, milestone completion history, or any other personal data as part of this request — only the title you typed and a small amount of app-generated context needed to shape a sensible trail (like how many camps you asked for).

Beta caveat, disclosed plainly: during Thaki's beta/TestFlight period, this feature may use a free-tier AI model. Free-tier models from some providers may log prompts for abuse monitoring or use them to improve their own models — this is a limitation of the underlying free service, not something Thaki does with your data on our own servers. We plan to move to a paid, no-logging model before a general App Store release; this policy will be updated when that happens, and this paragraph will be removed once it's no longer true.

Manual trail-building — pitching your own camps without asking the AI — never sends anything to this AI provider. It's always available and always free.

4. Crash reporting

If Thaki crashes or hits an unexpected error, we may send a crash report to Sentry, a crash-reporting service, so we can fix the bug. Crash reports can include technical details like a stack trace, app version, and device/OS type; they are not designed to and should not contain your goal titles, check-in notes, or other personal content, though no crash-reporting tool can make an absolute guarantee that no fragment of in-memory state ever appears in a report. Crash reporting is limited to what's necessary to diagnose the crash and is retained on Sentry's side only for a limited retention window, per our error-reporting provider's plan.

5. What we don't do

  • No ads. Thaki has no advertising SDKs, no ad networks, and no

ad-targeting profile of you.

  • No sale of data. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to

any third party, for any purpose, ever.

  • No data broker relationships. The only third-party processors

involved are the ones named in this policy (Supabase, OpenRouter, Sentry), each used strictly to provide the feature described above.

6. Your choices and controls

  • Notifications ("Gentle nudges") can be turned off entirely from

Settings, in addition to iOS's own notification controls. Turning this off cancels any scheduled reminders immediately.

  • Account deletion is available in-app, from Settings → Delete account.

This deletes your account and its server-side copy of your data; usage events (§2) are disconnected from your identity at that point and expire within our retention window (on the order of 180 days). You'll be asked whether to also wipe the local copy on that device, or keep it — your choice, either way, is respected exactly.

  • Export / import lets you take a full copy of your local data out of

the app at any time, independent of any account.

  • Sign-out never deletes your local data — it only stops that device

from syncing until you sign back in.

7. Children

Thaki does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age applicable in your jurisdiction, and does not include a birthday or age-gate flow. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us using the address below and we'll remove it.

8. Changes to this policy

If we materially change what we collect or how we use it, we'll update this page and adjust the "Last updated" date above. Continuing to use Thaki after a change means you accept the updated policy; if you don't, you can stop using the app's networked features (or the app entirely) and delete your account at any time.
