Thaki

Big goals, tiny steps

Thaki is an iPhone app for turning a big goal into milestones, and each milestone into tiny steps small enough to do today. Walk one a day, keep a quiet journal of the trail behind you, and, with Thaki Pro, ask Thaki to plan the goal with you. No feed, no ads, no tracking.
